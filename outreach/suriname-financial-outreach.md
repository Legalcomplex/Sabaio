## Three corrections to carry into the copy

1. **Suriname is NOT on the FATF grey list.** It was on neither the 13 Feb 2026 nor the 19 Jun 2026 increased-monitoring list. What is true, and sharper: Suriname is in **CFATF enhanced follow-up** from its 2023 Mutual Evaluation, with a 3rd Enhanced Follow-Up Report issued October 2025. Saying "grey list" to a Surinamese compliance officer costs the meeting.
2. **Suriname already recognises digitally signed documents as legally valid.** Since 7 April 2025 the Ministry of Home Affairs issues digital uittreksels and nationality declarations certified by "PDFsigner – e-Government Suriname" with a QR verification code, same legal status as the physical version, no wet stamp required. ([dbsuriname.com, 13 Jun 2025](https://www.dbsuriname.com/2025/06/13/digitale-documenten-suriname-volledig-rechtsgeldig/)) This is a government precedent worth putting in the first paragraph of every email — it turns "is this even legal here?" from the objection that kills the meeting into the reason the meeting happens.
3. **Landbouwbank N.V. no longer exists.** Absorbed into Volkscredietbank in 2015. It still appears in stale directories. Remove it from any list.

**No Surinamese financial institution publishes a named-executive email address.** Not one. Paid aggregators only expose masked stubs. Every named person below is `[NOT FOUND]` for a personal address — route via the generic inbox with the person named in the subject line, or via LinkedIn. Do not let a tool auto-generate `firstname.lastname@` and blast it.
