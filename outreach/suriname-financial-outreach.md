### Recommended route in

Email alone will not reach this board. Use a **three-track approach**:

1. **LinkedIn first** — Ashna Kamta (CRO) is the natural owner of a KYC/AML and counterparty-verification pitch; Raveen Koelfat (CCO), newly appointed and building a commercial agenda, is the warmer commercial door.
2. **`contactcenter@dsb.sr`** — a short, named, written request addressed to a specific director, asking to be routed. Weak on its own, useful as a paper trail.
3. **Phone +597 471 100** — ask for the directiesecretariaat by name. In Paramaribo this generally outperforms cold email.

### Angle

- DSB is mid-way through a **core banking and digital banking upgrade with Fiserv** — a bank already committed to replatforming is buying and integrating systems, and contract lifecycle plus counterparty onboarding sits directly adjacent to that programme.
- **No sitting CEO** cuts both ways: no single yes, but a CRO and a new CCO with unusual room to sponsor something themselves.
- The **oil and gas supplier wave** (GranMorgu / Block 58) lands on DSB as the country's largest bank: new corporate relationships to onboard, each needing KYC and AML on counterparties it has never seen before — exactly what Sabaio verifies at signature.
- Suriname's AML/CFT regime and its CFATF follow-up commitments keep counterparty verification a board-level obligation, not a back-office preference.

### Gaps

- `dsb.sr` and the 2025 annual report PDF could not be opened from this environment (network policy), so the directie page and any addresses printed in the report remain unchecked. **Worth one manual look at dsb.sr/nl/particulier/over-dsb/ and the jaarverslag** — annual reports often print a directiesecretariaat address.
