## The market story — use this in every email

Four things are true in Suriname right now, and together they are the entire pitch:

1. **The CFATF 5th evaluation round runs 2026–2027.** Suriname is in enhanced follow-up from its 2023 mutual evaluation; the 2nd Follow-Up Report (14 Oct 2024) brought re-rating upgrades on R.3, 5, 11, 19, 26, 27, 28 and 30. **Suriname is *not* on the FATF grey list** — and the private sector is afraid of ending up there. Saying "grey list" to a Surinamese compliance officer costs the meeting.
2. **The coordinating body was switched off.** The AML-PIU has been non-operational since **July 2025**. The SEOB's 28th bulletin (April 2026) and the VSB's National Risk Assessment alarm (May–June 2026) both warn that the lost coordination puts correspondent banking, international transactions and investor confidence at risk.
3. **29 laws and state decrees must be amended**, per NAMLAC and Justice Minister Harish Monorath (~Aug 2026) — covering supervision of money transfer offices, insurers, payment service providers, virtual asset providers, **and a legally anchored UBO register**. The Wet Toezicht Virtuele Activa Dienstverleners 2026 passed DNA 21–22 Aug 2026, 32 votes.
4. **GranMorgu is creating a vendor-onboarding wave.** TotalEnergies took FID Oct 2024 on a US$10.5bn development: >US$1bn local content, ~6,000 jobs, **45 participating sectors**, ~US$1.5bn of spend with Surinamese companies before first oil in 2028. Government has framed 2026 as the start of a national local-content programme.

**One sentence:** *the country's AML coordination capacity was withdrawn exactly as its evaluation deadline and its biggest-ever supplier-onboarding wave arrived — Sabaio is contract management with eSign, KYC and AML built in, grounded in Surinamese law.*

**And the precedent that removes the first objection:** since 7 April 2025 the Ministry of Home Affairs issues digital uittreksels and nationality declarations certified by "PDFsigner – e-Government Suriname" with a QR verification code, same legal status as the physical version, no wet stamp. ([dbsuriname.com, 13 Jun 2025](https://www.dbsuriname.com/2025/06/13/digitale-documenten-suriname-volledig-rechtsgeldig/)) Put it in paragraph one — it turns "is this even legal here?" from the objection that kills the meeting into the reason for it.
